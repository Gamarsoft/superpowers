Chosen direction: drawer-based export flow

Keep users on the report page and open a right-side drawer for format, scope, and destination, because the export choices stay explicit without sending people away from their report.

Evidence

  1. Keeping the report visible preserves the user’s place while they set format, scope, and destination.
  2. A right-side drawer makes the export choices explicit in one bounded flow instead of distributing them across a new screen.
  3. The settled direction is authored here and remains valid even if no browser click happened before this memo was created.
