# Packet Completion

Before handing off a newly finalized packet:

- Canonical packet.md marker, packet.json schema, current prototype/references and preserved history follow the [finalization recipe](packet-finalization.md). The read-only check passes; handoff carries revision and descriptor SHA-256.
- Follow-on entry links settled approved spec/context; standalone entry contains the sufficient brief and original design/implementation scope.
- Status is `approved`, or `approved-with-degraded-evidence` with explicit approval of listed limitations. Unresolved required direction stays `required-pending`.
- Brownfield notes distinguish constraints/anchors from problems/freedom and identify current versus historical evidence.
- Screen index names implementation-shaping screens/states, setup, viewports, observable acceptance, exact references/intents, and actual evidence revision. Earlier checks and missing proof are explicit.
- Selected concept/version, material refinement and human selection are recorded when exploration ran.
- Final prototype source/version, launch, synthetic-state setup, desktop/narrow captures, supersession and exercised interactions are recoverable when the prototype loop ran.
- Every binding target's intent/scope and final approval are clear. Missing intent blocks dependent visual changes; no duplicate approval ceremony for a classification already covered by packet approval.
- Copy source, responsive/accessibility constraints, concrete component mapping and production verification expectations are named. Validate visual/interaction quality separately from the file check.
- Historical approved artifacts remain usable; no fictitious retrofit of generation or prototype evidence.
- Return to the invoking workflow, or complete/continue the standalone request within its original authorization. No automatic standalone delivery router.
