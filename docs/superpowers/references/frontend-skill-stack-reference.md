# Frontend Skill Stack

`superpowers:frontend-direction` supports both an approved neutral spec/follow-on from brainstorming and standalone existing-screen improvement. For substantial unresolved direction it produces three image concepts, a refined selection, an exercised interactive prototype, and an approved packet with durable exact targets.

`superpowers:implementing-frontend-design` consumes approved packets, prototypes or explicit references inside the active implementation workflow. It preserves the chosen hierarchy/interactions and checks runtime fidelity with independent visual review for non-trivial work. It does not require GSD context or restart discovery.

Impeccable can supply focused assessment and refinement when installed in the target project or session. Both skills share its conditional integration guidance. No optional plugin is a prerequisite.

Business and accessibility requirements, retained brand/shell, and the approved feature design delta govern their own scopes. Use the canonical authority contract in `skills/frontend-direction/references/design-source-priority.md`; old UI is not automatic visual authority over the approved redesign.

New final packets use `--frontend/packet.md` as their sole human entry, `packet.json` schema1, a screen/state index, conditional brownfield inspection, current `prototype/` and `references/`, and nonbinding `history/`. The shared [finalization recipe](../../../skills/frontend-direction/references/packet-finalization.md) and read-only helper validate current-file identity, required mappings and matching approval revision. Handoffs carry the revision and descriptor SHA-256. Canonical signals require validation even when a descriptor goes missing; legacy inputs with no canonical signal keep their original checks.

A packet carries the brief/spec, reference intents (`visual-truth`, `semantic-guidance`, `reference-only`), exact selected versions, prototype source/launch/states, final desktop/narrow captures, supersession, copy and implementation verification. Temporary brainstorming companion sketches remain early decision context; the final approved prototype is retained evidence.

For brainstorming, the packet is approved before delivery routing and one selected adapter. Standalone design-only work ends with the approved target; authorized implementation continues without a mandatory route-selection ceremony. Mechanical edits and existing approved targets skip open exploration.

Migration: `gsd-frontend-design` is now `implementing-frontend-design`. Upload-pack authoring was retired in favor of built-in imagegen. Historical approved images, packets and `chatgpt-image-2/` paths remain readable with their original authority. Saved explicit invocations of retired skill names need updating; no alias is installed.
