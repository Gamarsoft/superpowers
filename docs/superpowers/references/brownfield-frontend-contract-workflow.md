# Brownfield Frontend Contract Workflow

Inspect the real feature to separate business/technical constraints, retained brand/shell and design problems. Reconsider weak feature composition and interaction while honoring those constraints.

Use frontend-direction directly for a bounded existing-screen improvement, or consume an approved neutral spec/follow-on after brainstorming. Substantial unresolved direction defaults to three actual image concepts, refinement of the human's selection, and a high-fidelity interactive prototype. Exercise important states, narrow layout and keyboard/focus, then retain the final approved version and captures in the packet.

The packet records scoped reference intent, copy, invariants, approved delta, prototype source/launch/state setup, supersession and verification expectations. Legacy packets keep their recorded intent; no retrospective redesign authority is invented.

After the packet is approved, a brainstorming continuation returns to its delivery route. Standalone work keeps its original design-only or implementation scope without a routing ceremony. Implement approved designs with implementing-frontend-design in the active lane, then compare runtime against exact targets and perform fresh visual review for non-trivial UI.

Fixture screenshots prove the states exercised, not backend integration. Missing evidence remains explicit; approved degraded constraints or waivers name the actual gap. Optional Impeccable supports critique/refinement without overriding the target or becoming a prerequisite.
