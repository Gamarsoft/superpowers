# Choir practice aid — first experiment

Purpose: help your mother start a rehearsal on her existing tablet without needing help.

She can choose one song, see a short practice prompt, mark today's attempt, and see her attempts over the last seven calendar days. A missed day is shown neutrally, with no failure message, score, or broken streak. The experience should feel calm and easy to follow.

The first delivery has no accounts, cloud sync, scores, or notifications. Practice records stay on the tablet. Losing those records is acceptable for this experiment.

Assumptions for this draft:

- Delivery will be a small browser-based aid opened on her tablet. Its browser supports local storage; compatibility with her actual tablet still needs checking.
- A small initial song list and prompts will be supplied for the experiment. Audio playback and sheet music are outside this first slice.
- “Today” and the seven-day view use the tablet's local calendar date. Marking today again does not create a second attempt for that day.

Acceptance examples:

- Choosing a song reveals a short prompt that lets her begin practicing.
- Marking today's attempt makes that day visibly recorded in the seven-day view; reopening the aid retains it while local storage is available.
- Missing yesterday leaves yesterday unmarked and today available, without criticism or a failure state.
- The experiment succeeds when she can open the aid and start a rehearsal without assistance.
