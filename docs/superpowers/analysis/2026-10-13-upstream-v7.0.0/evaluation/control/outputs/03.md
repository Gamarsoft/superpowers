## Choir practice aid

**Purpose:** Help your mother start a rehearsal on her existing tablet without needing help. The experience should feel calm and welcoming, including after missed days.

**First experiment:** She can choose one song, see a short practice prompt for it, mark today's attempt, and see her attempts over the last seven calendar days, including today. A day with no recorded attempt carries no failure message, score, or penalty.

**Boundary:** No accounts, cloud sync, scores, or notifications. Records stay on the tablet. Losing those records is acceptable for this experiment.

**Visible assumptions:** The tablet can open a browser; I will use a simple browser-based aid as the initial delivery choice. Its operating system is unknown, so compatibility with her actual tablet must be checked before calling it ready. The initial song choices and prompts are supplied in the aid; editing that content is outside this first slice. Marking an attempt twice on the same day does not create two daily records. The tablet's local date defines today.

**Acceptance examples:**

- She opens the aid, chooses a song, and sees a short prompt that lets her begin practicing.
- She marks today's attempt and sees it in the seven-day history. Repeating that action leaves one record for today.
- She returns after missing a day and can begin another rehearsal without a failure message or catch-up requirement.
- She closes and reopens the aid on the same tablet and sees her locally saved history, provided the tablet has retained its local data.

**Success:** She starts a rehearsal without needing help.
