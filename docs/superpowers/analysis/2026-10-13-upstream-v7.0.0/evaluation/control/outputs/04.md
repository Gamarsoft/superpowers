## B

**Purpose:** Help your mother start a choir rehearsal independently on the tablet she already owns.

**First experiment:** She chooses one song, sees a short practice prompt, marks today's attempt and sees her activity for the last seven days. A missed day is presented neutrally, without failure language or a broken streak.

**Boundaries:** No accounts, cloud sync, scores or notifications. Practice history stays on the tablet. Losing that local history is acceptable for this experiment.

**Visible assumptions:** I propose delivery through a tablet web browser, assuming her tablet has a suitable browser. Its operating system and browser have not been identified or verified. Songs and short prompts are supplied with the experiment; there is no song-management feature in this slice. One attempt marker per song per local calendar day is sufficient, and the seven-day view includes today. These are assumptions for review.

**Acceptance examples:**

- She can choose a song and begin using its practice prompt without help.
- Marking today's attempt updates the seven-day view; marking it again does not create a second daily marker.
- Returning on the same tablet retains her song activity while local storage remains available.
- A day without an attempt appears neutrally and does not prevent her from starting again.

**Success:** She starts a rehearsal without needing help. The implementation language and framework are delegated implementation choices.
